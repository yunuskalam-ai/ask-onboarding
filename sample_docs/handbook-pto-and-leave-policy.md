# Paid Time Off and Leave Policy

Owner: People team. Last reviewed: September 2026.

## Annual leave

- Full-time employees get **25 days of paid annual leave** per year, plus UK bank holidays.
- Part-time employees get the same entitlement pro rata to their contracted hours.
- The leave year runs from **1 January to 31 December**.
- After **3 years of service** you get one extra day per year, up to a maximum of **28 days** after 6 years.

## Requesting leave

Request all leave in **PeopleHub**. Your manager approves or declines it there.

- For up to 2 days off, request at least **1 week** in advance.
- For 3 or more consecutive days, request at least **4 weeks** in advance.
- Managers should reply to a request within 3 working days.

## Carrying over leave

You can carry over up to **5 unused days** into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by **31 March**, or they are lost. Unused days beyond 5 cannot be paid out, except when you leave the company.

## Sick leave

- You get **10 days of full company sick pay** per rolling 12 months. After that, Statutory Sick Pay applies.
- Tell your manager before 10:00 on your first day of sickness, by Slack or phone.
- Log sick days in PeopleHub when you are back.
- For absences up to **7 calendar days** you can self-certify. For longer absences you need a fit note from a doctor.

## Parental leave

- **Primary carers** (birth parent or primary adopter) get **20 weeks at full pay**, then statutory pay for the remaining statutory leave.
- **Secondary carers** get **6 weeks at full pay**, which can be taken in one block or in weekly blocks within the first year.
- You are eligible from your first day of employment.
- Tell the People team at least 15 weeks before the expected week of birth, or as soon as you can for adoption.

## Other leave

- **Compassionate leave:** up to 5 days paid for the death of a close family member, and up to 2 days for other family members. Talk to your manager; no paperwork is needed.
- **Jury service:** paid at full salary. Send your summons to people@yklabs.example.
- **Volunteering:** 2 paid volunteering days per year for a registered charity.
- **Moving house:** 1 paid day per year.
