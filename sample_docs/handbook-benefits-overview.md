# Benefits Overview

All permanent employees get these benefits from their **first day**, unless stated otherwise. Manage your benefits in PeopleHub under "My benefits".

## Pension

You are enrolled automatically in the company pension scheme.

- YK Labs pays **6% of your salary** if you pay in at least **4%**.
- If you pay in less than 4%, the company pays the statutory minimum of 3%.
- You can pay in through **salary sacrifice**, which saves National Insurance.
- Change your contribution in PeopleHub at any time. Changes made before the 15th of the month apply to that month's payroll.

## Health

- **Private medical insurance** through Healthwell, covering you and, if you choose, your partner and children. Cover for yourself is free. Cover for family members is a taxable benefit.
- **Dental cover** for you at no cost.
- **Free eye tests** and up to £75 towards glasses if you use a screen at work.
- **Employee Assistance Programme (EAP):** a free, confidential 24/7 helpline for counselling, legal and money advice. The phone number is on the benefits page in PeopleHub.

## Life assurance and income protection

- **Life assurance** of **4 times your annual salary**.
- **Income protection** pays 75% of your salary if you cannot work because of long-term illness, after 26 weeks of absence.

## Learning and development

Every employee has a **learning budget of £1,000 per year** for courses, books, conferences and certifications. Get your manager's approval before you book, then claim the cost through Spendly. Unused budget does not carry over to the next year.

## Other benefits

- **Cycle to work scheme:** buy a bike and accessories up to £2,500 through salary sacrifice.
- **Season ticket loan:** an interest-free loan for an annual train or travel card, paid back from your salary over 12 months.
- **Home office allowance** and **internet allowance:** see the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy.
- **Referral bonus:** £2,000 for referring someone we hire, paid after they pass probation.
