IT HOW-TO: RESETTING YOUR OKTA PASSWORD
Owner: IT team. Questions: #it-help on Slack.

Your Okta password signs you in to almost every company app, so keep it safe.

PASSWORD RULES
- At least 14 characters.
- Must not contain your name or username.
- Must not match any of your last 5 passwords.
- Okta asks you to change it every 12 months.
- Store it in 1Password, the company password manager.

IF YOU FORGOT YOUR PASSWORD
1. Go to larkspur.okta.example and click "Forgot password?".
2. Enter your work email address.
3. Approve the push notification in Okta Verify on your phone.
4. Choose a new password that follows the rules above.
5. Restart your laptop and sign in with the new password, so your laptop and Okta stay in sync.

IF YOUR ACCOUNT IS LOCKED
Okta locks your account for 30 minutes after 5 failed sign-in attempts.
You can wait 30 minutes and try again, or ask in #it-help to unlock it sooner.
IT will check who you are on a short video call before unlocking it.

IF YOU LOST YOUR PHONE (NO OKTA VERIFY)
You cannot reset your password yourself without Okta Verify.
1. Ask a colleague to post in #it-help for you, or call the IT desk on extension 4400.
2. IT checks your identity on a video call with your camera on.
3. IT gives you a temporary code that works for 1 hour.
4. Enrol Okta Verify on your new phone straight away.

IF YOU THINK SOMEONE KNOWS YOUR PASSWORD
Change it immediately using the steps above, then report it to security@larkspur.example within 1 hour.
This counts as a security incident, but you will not get in trouble for reporting it.

IT WILL NEVER ASK FOR YOUR PASSWORD
Nobody from IT will ever ask for your password or your Okta Verify code, by email, phone or Slack.
If someone does, it is a phishing attempt. Report it with the "Report Phish" button or to security@larkspur.example.
