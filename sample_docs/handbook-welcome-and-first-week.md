# Welcome to YK Labs

YK Labs builds Routewise, scheduling software that helps logistics companies plan deliveries. We are about 300 people across our London head office, our Manchester office and remote staff in the UK.

## Your first day

- Arrive at the London office at **9:30** on your first day. The address is 14 Cobalt Yard, London EC2A 4XY. Ask for the People team at reception on the ground floor.
- If you are joining remotely, your laptop is couriered to arrive at least two working days before your start date. Your first call starts at 9:30 on Google Meet; the link is in your personal email.
- Your **IT setup session** is at **11:00** on day one. IT will help you sign in to Okta, set up multi-factor authentication and install the core apps.
- Lunch on day one is with your team and your onboarding buddy.

## Your onboarding buddy

Every new joiner gets an onboarding buddy from a different team. Your buddy is not your manager. They are the person to ask "silly" questions: where things are, who owns what, and how things really work. Meet your buddy at least once a week for your first four weeks.

## Your first week checklist

1. Sign in to Okta and enrol in Okta Verify for multi-factor authentication.
2. Join the Slack channels #general, #announcements, #it-help and your team channel.
3. Complete your profile in PeopleHub, our HR system: emergency contact, bank details and address.
4. Complete the **security awareness training** in PeopleHub. This is mandatory and must be finished within **14 days** of your start date.
5. Read the Code of Conduct (the Employee Handbook PDF) and confirm you have read it in PeopleHub.
6. Book a 1:1 with your manager to agree your 30-60-90 day goals.

## Check-ins and probation

Your manager holds check-ins at **30, 60 and 90 days**. Most roles have a **probation period of 3 months**. Leadership roles (manager level and above) have a probation period of **6 months**. During probation the notice period is one week on either side.
