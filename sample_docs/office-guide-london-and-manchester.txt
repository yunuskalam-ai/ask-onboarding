OFFICE GUIDE: LONDON AND MANCHESTER
Owner: Workplace team (office@yklabs.example)

=== LONDON (HEAD OFFICE) ===

Address: 14 Cobalt Yard, London EC2A 4XY
Nearest stations: Old Street (5 minutes walk) and Liverpool Street (12 minutes walk).
We use floors 3 and 4. Reception is on the ground floor.

Opening hours: 7:00 to 21:00, Monday to Friday. The building is closed at weekends and on UK bank holidays.

Keycards: you get your keycard from reception on your first day. Lost keycards cost nothing to replace the first time. Tell office@yklabs.example straight away so the old card can be switched off.

Desks: desks are not assigned. Book a desk in the "Desks" app (open it from Okta). Teams have neighbourhoods: Engineering on floor 4, everyone else on floor 3. Clear your desk at the end of the day; lockers are next to the kitchen on floor 3.

Kitchen and food: tea, coffee, fruit and snacks are free every day. Breakfast is provided on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 8:30 to 10:00. Lunch is provided on Thursdays at 12:30.

Bikes and parking: secure bike storage is in the basement, with 40 spaces and showers. There is no car parking at the office. The nearest public car park is on Tabernacle Street.

Visitors: register visitors in the "Desks" app at least 24 hours before they arrive. Visitors must sign in at reception and wear a visitor badge. You must meet your visitor at reception.

Fire safety: the fire alarm is tested every Wednesday at 11:00; you do not need to leave for the test. In a real alarm, leave by the stairs (never the lifts) and go to the assembly point in Cobalt Square, opposite the main entrance. Fire wardens wear yellow vests.

First aid: first aid kits are in both kitchens. Trained first aiders are listed on the board next to reception.

=== MANCHESTER ===

Address: 2nd floor, 88 Mill Lane, Manchester M1 3FZ
Nearest station: Manchester Piccadilly (8 minutes walk).
Opening hours: 8:00 to 19:00, Monday to Friday.

The Manchester office has 40 desks. Book them in the same "Desks" app. Tea and coffee are free; lunch is provided on Tuesdays at 12:30. There are 6 bike spaces in the courtyard and no car parking.

Fire assembly point: the car park at the back of the building on Mill Lane.

=== LOST PROPERTY ===
Hand in anything you find at reception. Unclaimed items are given to charity after 30 days.
