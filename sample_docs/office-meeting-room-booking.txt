MEETING ROOMS: HOW TO BOOK
Owner: Workplace team (office@larkspur.example)

HOW TO BOOK
Book meeting rooms in Google Calendar. Create an event, click "Rooms" and choose a room.
Rooms are named after birds.

BOOKING RULES
- You can book a room for up to 2 hours at a time.
- Osprey (the boardroom) needs approval from the office manager. Email office@larkspur.example with the date, time and reason.
- If nobody checks in on the room screen within 10 minutes of the start time, the booking is released automatically.
- Do not book a room for a recurring meeting for more than 3 months ahead.
- Phone booths on each floor cannot be booked. Use them for up to 30 minutes.

VIDEO CALLS
Kestrel, Osprey, Swift and Lark have Google Meet hardware. Add the room to your calendar event and tap "Join" on the room's touch panel.
If the equipment does not work, post in #it-help with the room name.

AFTER YOUR MEETING
Wipe the whiteboard, take your cups to the kitchen and leave the room on time for the next booking.
