# Information Security Policy

Owner: Security team (security@larkspur.example). This policy applies to all employees and contractors.

## Data classification

Every piece of company information has one of four labels.

| Label | Examples | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public | Marketing site, published blog posts | Can be shared with anyone |
| Internal | Handbook, team plans, most Slack messages | Employees and contractors only |
| Confidential | Contracts, salary data, product roadmaps | Shared only with people who need it |
| Restricted | Customer data, production credentials, personal data | Only through approved systems; access is logged |

Customer data is always **Restricted**. Never copy it to a personal device, a personal email account or an AI tool that the Security team has not approved.

## Accounts and passwords

- Use **Okta** to sign in to company apps. Multi-factor authentication with **Okta Verify** is required for every account.
- Passwords must be at least **14 characters** long. Use the company password manager, 1Password, for every password.
- Never share your password or your MFA codes, including with IT. IT will never ask for them.

## Devices

- Only company-managed laptops may access Confidential or Restricted data.
- Your screen must lock after **5 minutes** of inactivity. This is set automatically on company laptops.
- **USB storage devices are blocked** on company laptops. Use Google Drive to share files.
- Keep your laptop updated. Install security updates within **7 days** of being prompted.

## Phishing

If an email looks suspicious, do not click links or open attachments. Use the **"Report Phish" button** in Gmail, or forward the email to security@larkspur.example. The Security team sends practice phishing emails every month.

## Reporting a security incident

Report any suspected security incident **within 1 hour** of noticing it. Examples: a lost or stolen laptop, a password you typed into a fake site, or customer data sent to the wrong person.

- Post in **#security-incidents** on Slack, or
- Email security@larkspur.example, or
- Out of hours, call the security on-call number pinned in #security-incidents.

You will not be punished for reporting a mistake quickly. Hiding a mistake is a disciplinary matter.

## Approved AI tools

Only AI tools listed on the Security team's Notion page are approved for company use. You must not paste Confidential or Restricted data into any other AI tool.
