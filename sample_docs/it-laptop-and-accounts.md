# IT: Laptops and Accounts

The IT team looks after your laptop, your accounts and the tools you use every day.

## Getting help

- Slack: **#it-help** (fastest)
- Email: it-help@yklabs.example
- Help desk portal: help.yklabs.example
- IT desk on the **3rd floor** of the London office

The IT desk is staffed **Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 18:00 UK time**. Outside these hours, only urgent security issues are handled: see the Information Security Policy.

## Your laptop

| Role | Standard laptop |
| --- | --- |
| Engineering and Data | MacBook Pro 14", 32 GB memory |
| All other roles | MacBook Air 13", 16 GB memory |

- Laptops are replaced every **3 years**. IT contacts you when yours is due.
- Need a Windows laptop for a specific reason? Ask your manager to raise a request in the help desk portal.
- Install apps from the **Self Service** app. Apps that are not in Self Service need approval from IT.
- You can borrow a monitor, keyboard, mouse or headset for home. Ask in #it-help.

## Accounts you get on day one

- **Okta**: single sign-on for everything below
- **Google Workspace**: email, calendar, Drive and Meet
- **Slack**
- **Notion**: team pages and documentation
- **Jira**: tickets and planning
- **PeopleHub**: HR, leave and payslips
- **Spendly**: expenses
- **1Password**: password manager
- Engineers also get **GitHub** and **PagerDuty**

Need another tool? Request it in the help desk portal. Your manager approves it, and IT sets it up within **2 working days**.

## Lost or stolen laptop

Report a lost or stolen laptop **within 1 hour** in #it-help and to security@yklabs.example. IT can lock and wipe the laptop remotely. If it was stolen, also report it to the police and send IT the crime reference number.

## Leaving the company

Return your laptop and any borrowed equipment on your last day. Remote employees get a prepaid returns box. Your accounts are switched off at **18:00 on your last working day**.
