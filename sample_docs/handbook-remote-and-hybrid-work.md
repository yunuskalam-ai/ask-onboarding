# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Larkspur Labs is a hybrid company. Most people work from an office **2 days a week** and from home the rest of the time.

## Anchor days

Our anchor days are **Tuesday and Thursday**. Teams plan workshops, planning sessions and socials on these days, so try to be in the office on them. If your team prefers different days, agree it with your manager and write it in your team's Notion page.

## Fully remote roles

Some roles are fully remote. A fully remote contract is agreed at hiring, or later with approval from your manager and your department head. Fully remote employees must still live in the UK and are expected to visit an office **once a quarter** for team planning. The company pays reasonable travel costs for these visits under the Expenses and Travel Policy.

## Working from abroad

You can work from another country for up to **20 working days per calendar year**.

- Ask your manager and the People team at least **2 weeks** before you travel.
- Some countries are not allowed for tax and security reasons. The People team checks your destination.
- You must use the VPN at all times while working abroad.
- Customer data marked Restricted must not be accessed from outside the UK.

## Home office setup

- A one-time **home office allowance of £400** for a desk, chair, monitor or other equipment. Claim it through Spendly within your first 3 months.
- An **internet allowance of £30 per month**, paid with your salary.
- IT can also lend you a monitor and keyboard. Ask in #it-help.

## Working hours

Core hours are **10:00 to 16:00 UK time**, when everyone should be reachable. Outside core hours you can arrange your day with your team. Set your working hours in Google Calendar so colleagues know when you are available.
