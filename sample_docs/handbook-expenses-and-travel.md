# Expenses and Travel Policy

Owner: Finance. All expenses are submitted and approved in **Spendly**.

## The basic rules

1. Spend company money as if it were your own.
2. Submit each expense within **30 days** of the purchase date. Late claims need Finance approval.
3. Attach a receipt for every expense over **£25**. For smaller amounts a short description is enough.
4. Your manager approves your expenses in Spendly.

## When you get paid back

Expenses approved by the **20th of the month** are paid back with that month's salary. Expenses approved after the 20th are paid with the following month's salary.

## Travel

| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Trains | Standard class. First class only if it costs less than standard. |
| Flights under 6 hours | Economy |
| Flights of 6 hours or more | Premium economy, with your manager's approval |
| Hotels in London | Up to **£200 per night** |
| Hotels elsewhere in the UK | Up to **£140 per night** |
| Hotels abroad | Up to **£180 per night**, or the Finance country rate if higher |
| Taxis | Only when public transport is not practical, or after 21:00 |
| Mileage in your own car | 45p per mile |

Book flights and hotels through **TravelDesk**, our booking tool, which you can open from Okta. Bookings outside TravelDesk need Finance approval first.

## Meals

- Meals while travelling in the UK: up to **£35 per day**.
- Meals while travelling abroad: up to **£60 per day**.
- Alcohol is not reimbursed, except at team events approved by a department head.
- Team meals and client entertainment need approval from your manager before the event.

## Company cards

Managers and people who travel often can request a company card from Finance. Every card payment still needs a receipt uploaded in Spendly within 30 days. Personal spending on a company card is not allowed, even if you pay it back.

## What is not reimbursed

Fines (for example parking or speeding fines), personal entertainment, upgrades, and lost-luggage costs that the airline should cover.
